# Part V: ledger, Double-Entry Bookkeeping

*We build `ledger`, a small plain-text accounting engine and command-line tool in the spirit of ledger-cli, hledger and beancount: money that can't be added to the wrong currency, transactions that cannot exist unless they balance, and reports that are nothing more than folds over a journal. It is the purest domain-driven project in the book, and it adds phantom types, aggregates that guard their invariants, typestate builders and reports-as-folds to the toolkit.*
